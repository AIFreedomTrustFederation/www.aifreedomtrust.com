01 · HUMAN AGENCY Tree: Roots

Humanity remains the author

The Federation begins with an ethical commitment: human dignity, consent, conscience, and the ability of people and communities to decide how intelligence is used.

The homepage calls these commitments Humanity Remains the Author, Sovereignty Should Serve Life, and Build What Repairs. They establish the standards by which work should be judged, not evidence that every connected technology meets them.

Source and evidence boundary. Published commitment: human dignity, consent, self-determination, and life-serving work. A mission statement does not certify a product, legal status, or deployment.

02 · SHARED COVENANT Tree: Trunk

Many works. One accountable purpose.

The trunk represents a shared covenant among distinct projects. The Federation is described as a relationship among independently responsible works rather than a single machine or institution.

The operating model describes a layered system in which each work is expected to state its purpose, role, maturity, and verification limits. The covenant concerns coordination without erasing local responsibility.

Source and evidence boundary. Documented model: distinct works sharing a purpose. Shared language alone does not demonstrate authenticated integration, independent oversight, or operational authority.

The published map describes Public Trust, Infrastructure, Applied Systems, Stewardship and Value, and Incubation or Legacy work. These are different responsibilities, not interchangeable products.

Source and evidence boundary. Documented organization: named functions and maturity labels. Inclusion in a layer is not proof of a working service, approved governance, or completed integration.
